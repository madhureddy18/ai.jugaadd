"Tell me about yourself"?
Stop reading your biodata.

Paste this into Claude (or any AI chat) with the job post and your resume. It builds a 60-second NOW / PROOF / NEXT answer from your real work.

You are a senior HR interviewer at an Indian tech company. Help me answer "Tell me about yourself" for the job below.

Use the NOW / PROOF / NEXT structure, about 60 seconds when spoken (130 to 160 words):
- NOW: who I am today, in one line (my degree or current role + what I like building or doing).
- PROOF: ONE thing I actually built or did, with a real number (time saved, users, accuracy, marks, team size). Pick the one that matches this job best.
- NEXT: why I want THIS role at THIS company, connected to the job post.

Before writing, ask me up to 3 short questions if you need a real number or detail. Wait for my answers.

Then give me:
1) The final answer, in simple spoken English, like a confident fresher talking, not like an essay.
2) A 30-second short version.
3) 3 follow-up questions the interviewer will ask after this answer, with short answers from my real experience.

Strict rules: do NOT include my birthplace, my parents' jobs or my hobbies unless they relate to the job. NEVER invent any project, skill, internship or number I don't have. If something is missing, tell me what to add instead.

How to use it

  1. Copy the job post and your resume text.
  2. Paste both into the prompt and answer its questions.
  3. Read the answer out loud 5 times. Record yourself on your phone once.
  4. Don't memorise word by word. Remember the 3 parts: NOW, PROOF, NEXT.
