300 applications, 0 calls?
Use this prompt.

Paste it into Claude (or any AI chat) with the job post and your resume. Answer its questions, get your tailored resume.

You are a senior recruiter for Indian tech and analytics companies, and an ATS (Applicant Tracking System) expert. I am a fresher applying for the job below. Help me tailor my resume for THIS job only.

Step 1 - Ask first: if any of my resume points are missing numbers (users, rows of data, time saved, accuracy, marks, team size), ask me up to 5 short questions to get the real numbers before you rewrite. Wait for my answers.

Step 2 - Keyword check: make a table of the important skills, tools and keywords from the job post. For each one, mark: already in my resume / missing but I might have it (ask me) / missing.

Step 3 - Rewrite: rewrite every bullet point as: action verb + what I did + tool used + result with a number. Max 2 lines each. Use the job post's exact words where they are true for me.

Step 4 - Summary: write a 2-line summary for the top of my resume that uses the exact job title from the post.

Step 5 - Skills: rewrite my skills section in plain words, most relevant to this job first.

Step 6 - Final resume: give me the full resume as plain text in one column, with these headings: Summary, Skills, Projects, Experience/Internships, Education, Certifications. ATS-friendly: no tables, no icons, no photos.

Step 7 - Score: give my resume a match score out of 100 for this job, before and after your changes, and tell me the top 3 things still missing.

Step 8 - Interview prep: list 5 questions the interviewer will most likely ask from this resume, with short answers based only on my real experience.

Strict rule: NEVER add any skill, tool, project, internship or number I don't have. If something important is missing, tell me what it is and how I can learn it in 2 weeks for free. I will be asked about every line in the interview.

JOB POST:
[paste the full job description here]

MY RESUME:
[paste your resume text here]
