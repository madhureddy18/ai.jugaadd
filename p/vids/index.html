Make a movie with one sentence

Google Vids turns one line into a 15-second video with sound. Free with a Google account.

✅ Free limit: 3 AI videos a month, up to 15 seconds each, with a normal Google account. Make each one count, so use the prompts below.
  1. On a laptop or computer, open vids.new and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Find the AI video option (it says something like "Create AI video" or "Generate video").
  3. Paste one prompt from below. Pick the length (up to 15 seconds).
  4. Press generate and wait a minute. Download it, then post it.
  5. Tip: say the camera ("slow motion", "drone shot", "close-up") and the sound ("crowd cheering", "thunder") in your line.

Google's free allowance can depend on your account and country, and may change. AI videos carry an invisible watermark that marks them as AI-made. Follow @maddiee.makess for more.
