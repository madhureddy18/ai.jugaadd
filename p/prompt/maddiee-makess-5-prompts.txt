MADDIE MAKES · THE 5 PROMPTS (+ PRO PROMPT)
@maddiee.makess · Make AI reels with Claude, no editing skills needed

HOW TO USE
1. Do them in order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5.
2. Paste each prompt into the Claude app (claude.ai or the mobile app).
3. Fill in everything in [brackets] with your own details.
4. Carry Claude's answer into the next prompt.
5. Prompt 3 gives you a playable 3D reel: open it full screen, screen-record, add voice/music in CapCut, post.

======================================================================

PROMPT 1 · Find your niche + 30 reel ideas
----------------------------------------------------------------------
I want to start an Instagram page that posts short AI-made skits (reels). Help me pick a niche and give me reel ideas.

About me: [what I know / what I like / my city / my language]
Who I want to reach: [students, freshers, office people, moms, gamers…]

1. Suggest 5 niches that are popular but not overcrowded, and why each one works.
2. For the best niche (or the one I pick), give me 30 reel ideas. For each: the relatable situation in one line + the twist.
3. Mark the 5 ideas with the strongest hook.

======================================================================

PROMPT 2 · Write the script (hook → twist → punchline)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Write a 25-second reel script for this idea: [paste one idea]

Characters: [main character, e.g. a toy-style boy named ___] and [sidekick, e.g. a small orange robot]
Structure:
- Hook (0–2s): the most relatable moment first
- Build-up: what goes wrong (with one visual gag)
- Twist: the surprise
- Punchline: one short funny line
- CTA: "Comment ___" (ONE word)

Give me, scene by scene: what we SEE, the dialogue (short lines), the on-screen text, and a one-line caption hook.

======================================================================

PROMPT 3 · Make the 3D reel (Claude builds it, you screen-record it)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Make me a 3D animated skit from my script for an Instagram Reel, as a single HTML page (artifact) using Three.js.

Characters:
- [Main character: a cute toy-style boy named ___, big round head, dark hair, navy t-shirt]
- [Sidekick: a small orange boxy robot with round eyes and an antenna]

Setting: [a cozy bedroom at night: bed, window with city lights, wall clock, lamp]

My script (about 20–25 seconds):
[paste the script from step 2]

Rules:
- Vertical 9:16 frame that fills the whole screen (no black bars).
- Characters move: walk, turn their heads, small gestures; mouths move when they talk.
- Big readable captions in the middle of the screen (not at the very bottom), timed to the story.
- At least 3 different camera shots with smooth moves.
- Simple motion graphics: pop-up cards, a clock or counter, emoji tags.
- A big ▶ Play button that starts it from the beginning, and a ⟲ Replay button. Hide buttons while it plays.
- No sound (I'll add voice and music later).
- Build everything from code shapes (no image files).

======================================================================

PROMPT 4 · Caption, hashtags + best time to post
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Here's my reel script: [paste the script]

Write:
1. 3 caption options. First line is a hook, max 2 short paragraphs, end with one question that makes people comment.
2. 5 hashtags (mix of big and niche ones).
3. A first comment to pin.
4. The best time to post for an Indian audience, weekdays vs weekends.
5. A cover title in 3–5 words.

======================================================================

PROMPT 5 · Your 30-day posting plan
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Make me a 30-day posting plan for my Instagram page about [niche].

- I can post [1 reel a day / 4 a week].
- For each day: the idea, the hook line, and the comment keyword.
- Reuse formats that work: every 7 days, repeat the best format with a new situation.
- Add a weekly check: which numbers to look at (views, shares, saves, follows), what's good for a new page, and what to change if a reel flops.

======================================================================

PRO PROMPT · fully automatic, with AI voices
(needs Claude Code, or a Claude chat that can create files and run code)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
You are my reel studio. Make a finished vertical video (1080x1920, 30 fps, MP4) of a 3D animated skit, rendered from code.

Tools: build the scene in Three.js, render it frame by frame in headless Chrome (Playwright), and join frames + audio with ffmpeg. Voices: use OmniVoice (free, open source): design each character's voice once from a description, then clone it for every line so they always sound the same. Add emotion tags like [sigh], [laughter], [surprise-oh] inside the lines.

Characters: [main character description] and [sidekick description]. Keep their look identical in every shot.
Voices: [main character: warm, young] · [sidekick: dry, slightly British].
Setting: [room / place, time of day, lighting mood].

Script (about 30s):
[HOOK line + what we see]
[BUILD-UP: what happens, with a visual gag]
[TWIST line]
[PUNCHLINE line]
[CTA: "Comment ___" (one word)]

Make it look premium:
- Fill the full 9:16 frame, no black bars. Hook in the first 2 seconds.
- Motion graphics drawn on top (cards, counters, labels) that never cover faces.
- Burned-in subtitles a little above the middle-bottom (above Instagram's buttons), speaker-coloured, 2–4 words at a time.
- Real camera work: wide, close-ups, slow push-ins; mouths move with the voice.
- Light background music that ducks under speech, plus small sound effects (pops, whooshes, ticks).
Before rendering the full video, show me still frames of the key moments so we can fix framing first.

======================================================================

RULES THAT GROW A PAGE
- Hook first: the most relatable moment in the first 2 seconds.
- Same characters every time: people follow characters, not one-off videos.
- One-word CTA: "Comment PROMPT", not a sentence.
- Post daily for 30 days before you judge the page.
