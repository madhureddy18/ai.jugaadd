YOU.

A documentary

Your phone already knows exactly how you spend your day. Hand that to an AI, and it narrates your life like a wildlife film: funny, a little brutal, and weirdly useful.

SPECIES
Engineerus graduatus
Active hours: 1 AM – 5 AM

How it works

1. Screenshot your screen time for today or this week (where to find it is below).
2. Copy the prompt and paste it into Claude (or any AI chat that takes images).
3. Attach the screenshot and add one line about yourself (student, fresher, job, what you meant to do today).

The prompt

You are a dry, slightly British wildlife-documentary narrator. I've attached a screenshot of my phone's screen time. Narrate my life as a short nature documentary about a rare species: me.

About me: [student / fresher / job, and what I planned to do today]

Rules:
- Use ONLY what's in the screenshot and what I told you: real app names, real hours, real pickups or unlocks. Don't invent facts.
- Deadpan and funny, never mean. Short lines with pauses, like a narrator reading slowly.
- Format:
  SPECIES: a fake Latin name for me
  HABITAT: where I clearly spend my time
  ACTIVE HOURS: based on the data
  Then 6–8 lines of narration.
  OBSERVED BEHAVIOUR: my single most surprising habit from the data
- End with one honest line out of character: the ONE change that would give me back the most time tomorrow, and roughly how many hours a week it frees.

Where to find your screen time

Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls → the dashboard (tap the chart for app-by-app time). Samsung: Settings → Digital Wellbeing.
iPhone: Settings → Screen Time → See All App & Website Activity (switch between Day and Week).

What you'll get (example)

SPECIES: Scrollus infinitus.

Here we observe the subject, awake at 1:12 AM. He has opened Instagram eighty-four times today. He has opened the job portal… once.

Six hours and nine minutes of screen time. The subject describes this as "just checking something."

OBSERVED BEHAVIOUR: unlocks the phone, stares, locks it again. Forty times.

Out of character: put Instagram on a 45-minute daily timer. That's about 20 hours a week back.
