10 apps you can replicate for India

Apps already earning online, and how to rebuild each one for Indian users.

✅ The rule: copy the idea, never the code, the name, the logo or the design. Build your own version for Indian users.
  1. Pick one that matches people you know (students, shops, creators).
  2. Check demand first: ask 10 people, or post a reel with a waitlist.
  3. Build a tiny first version with Claude using the starter prompt.
  4. Charge in rupees with UPI (Razorpay or a UPI link). Start small: ₹99–₹499.
  5. Get 10 paying users. Then improve.
01

🎨 Idea → app designs

The original: An app that turns a typed idea into app screen designs.$20,109 MRR

🇮🇳 India version: Same thing, but you can describe the idea in Hindi or Telugu, and it outputs simple screens for Indian small businesses (kirana, tuition, salon).

👥 Students, freelancers, small business owners💰 ₹299 / month

Starter prompt for Claude
Build a web app where a user types an app idea in Hindi, Telugu or English and gets 4–6 mobile screen designs (HTML/Tailwind) they can download. Keep it simple, mobile-first, with a free tier of 2 designs and UPI payment for more.
02

💬 Captions for videos

The original: Auto-captions for videos in 55+ languages.$3.6k MRR

🇮🇳 India version: Captions for Instagram reels in Hindi, Telugu, Tamil and Hinglish, in the bold styles Indian creators use, burned into the video.

👥 Reel creators, coaching pages, local businesses💰 ₹199 / month

Starter prompt for Claude
Build a web app that takes a short vertical video, transcribes it (Hindi/Telugu/Hinglish supported), and burns in bold animated captions in 3 styles. Free: 3 videos a month. Show a preview before download.
03

📰 Newsletter in your voice

The original: Drafts newsletter issues in the writer's own voice.$3.1k revenue (30 days)

🇮🇳 India version: Writes WhatsApp Channel / Telegram posts in the creator's own style, in Hindi or English, from a few notes.

👥 Teachers, coaches, local news pages, creators💰 ₹249 / month

Starter prompt for Claude
Build a tool where a creator pastes 5 of their old posts, then types rough notes; it writes a new WhatsApp Channel post in their style and language. Add a 'make it shorter' and 'add Hinglish' button.

📈 Trading journal

The original: A trading journal plus trade copier.$7.3k MRR

🇮🇳 India version: A simple trading journal for Indian F&O and stock traders: import the broker's trade file, see win rate, biggest mistakes, best days. Journal only, no tips.

👥 Retail traders💰 ₹299 / month

Starter prompt for Claude
Build a web app where a trader uploads their broker trade report (CSV), and it shows win rate, average profit/loss, best and worst days, and lets them tag each trade with a mistake. No buy/sell advice.

🧪 Lab report explainer

The original: Digitizes paper lab results and builds a health roadmap.$550 MRR

🇮🇳 India version: Explains blood test reports in simple Telugu, Hindi or English and tracks values over time. It explains; it doesn't diagnose, and it says to see a doctor.
