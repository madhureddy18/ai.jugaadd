Applied? Now message the HR.

Your application is one of 2,000. A short message to the person who's hiring makes them open it. Apply first, then send this.

1. Apply on the portal like normal. HR can only shortlist you if you're in their system.
2. Find who posted it (see the list below).
3. Send 4 lines: "I just applied" + their name + one proof + one ask.

The prompt

Paste it into Claude (or any AI chat) with the job post and your resume.

You are a senior tech recruiter in India. I have ALREADY APPLIED to the job below. Help me write a short message to the recruiter or hiring manager so they open my application.

Recruiter's name: [their first name, or "unknown"]
Where I'm messaging them: [LinkedIn / Naukri / Instahyre / Wellfound / email]

Write these 3 things:

1) MAIN MESSAGE (4 lines, under 80 words):
- Line 1: Hi [name], I just applied for the [exact job title] role at [company].
- Line 2: ONE proof from my resume that matches this job, with a real number (time saved, users, accuracy, marks, team size).
- Line 3: ONE clear ask (10 minutes to talk, or a quick look at my application).
- Line 4: Resume attached / thank you.

2) LINKEDIN CONNECTION NOTE: the same idea in under 200 characters.

3) FOLLOW-UP for 5 days later if they don't reply: 2 lines, polite, adds one new small detail.

Rules:
- Simple, friendly, confident English. Sound like a real fresher, not a template.
- NO "Respected Sir/Madam", NO "kindly consider my profile", NO "I am writing to express my interest".
- NEVER invent any project, skill, internship or number I don't have. If my resume has no number, ask me 2 short questions first and wait for my answers.
- Pick the proof that matches the job post best, not just my first project.

JOB POST:
[paste the job description here]

MY RESUME:
[paste your resume text here]

Where to find the HR / recruiter

  • LinkedIn: search "Company name + Talent Acquisition" or "+ HR". Also check the job post: "Meet the hiring team" shows who posted it.
  • Naukri: open the job and scroll to "Recruiter details" / "About company". Many posts show the recruiter's name.
  • Instahyre / Cutshort: the recruiter's name is on the job. Message them in the app after applying.
  • Wellfound (startups): message the founder or hiring lead shown on the job.

Small rules that work

  1. Apply first, then message the same day.
  2. One message per job. Use their name, never "Dear Hiring Manager".
  3. No reply in 5 days? Send the follow-up once. Then move on.
  4. Do it for the 5 jobs you want most each day, not 300.
Never: "Respected Sir, kindly consider my profile." Four real lines beat one long request.
