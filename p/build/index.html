- [Sidekick: a small orange boxy robot with round eyes and an antenna]

Setting: [a cozy bedroom at night: bed, window with city lights, wall clock, lamp]

The skit (about 20 seconds):
1. Hook (0–3s): [the relatable moment, e.g. "I'll sleep at 11"]
2. Build-up (3–11s): [what goes wrong, e.g. time-lapse: clock spins, robot paces]

Rules:

- Characters move: walk, turn their heads, small gestures; mouths move when they talk.
- Big readable captions in the middle of the screen (not at the very bottom), timed to the story.
- At least 3 different camera shots with smooth moves.
- Simple motion graphics: pop-up cards, a clock or counter, emoji tags.
- A big ▶ Play button that starts it from the beginning, and a ⟲ Replay button. Hide buttons while it plays.
- No sound (I'll add voice and music later).
- Build everything from code shapes (no image files).

Make it look premium:
- Fill the full 9:16 frame, no black bars. Hook in the first 2 seconds.
- Motion graphics drawn on top (cards, counters, labels) that never cover faces.
- Burned-in subtitles a little above the middle-bottom (above Instagram's buttons), speaker-coloured, 2–4 words at a time.
- Real camera work: wide, close-ups, slow push-ins; mouths move with the voice.
- Light background music that ducks under speech, plus small sound effects (pops, whooshes, ticks).
